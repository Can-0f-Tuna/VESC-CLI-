# TorqueBoards (DIY Electric Skateboard) 6374/6380 Motors

## Overview

TorqueBoards motors, sold through DIY Electric Skateboard (DIYEB), are premium-grade brushless outrunners designed specifically for electric skateboard applications. These motors feature extensive upgrades over standard OEM motors, including battle hardening, upgraded hall sensors, additional bearings, and CNC vent holes. They are designed and specified in-house with quality control that rivals the best in the industry.

**Manufacturer:** DIY Electric Skateboard (TorqueBoards brand)  
**Location:** Dallas, Texas, USA  
**Official Website:** https://diyelectricskateboard.com  
**Primary Applications:** High-performance electric skateboards, mountainboards

## TorqueBoards V2.0 Motor Features

### Maximum Stator Size
- Motors designed with largest possible stators for their can size
- More torque and power in compact package
- Shortened motor length without sacrificing performance

### Battle Hardened Plus
- **Epoxy Bonding:** All magnets factory-epoxied to prevent loosening
- **Mechanical Retainers:** Front and back retainers prevent magnet shift
- **Long-term Reliability:** Prevents magnet failures common in high-mileage motors

### CNC Vent Holes
- Precision-machined ventilation holes
- Improves cooling efficiency
- Strategic placement to minimize debris entry
- Optional mesh screen accessory available

### Upgraded Hall Sensors (V2.0)
- **Embedded Design:** Hall sensors placed in stator slots, not PCB
- **Accuracy:** 1-3° angle difference (vs 10-20° in older designs)
- **Reliability:** Much less prone to failure
- **VESC Performance:** Better FOC operation with precise sensor timing

### High Temperature Magnets
- Premium high-temp neodymium magnets
- Rated for high-performance thermal conditions
- Better than budget motors using lower-grade magnets

### Rubber Sealed Bearings
- **Upgraded from metal shielded:** Better contamination protection
- **Quantity:** 5+ bearings in larger motors (vs 2 in standard motors)
- **Longevity:** Prevents lubricant loss and dirt ingress
- **Maintenance:** Lower maintenance requirements

### Upgraded Drive Shaft
- **4-Point Retention System:** Prevents shaft-can separation
- **Problem Solved:** Addresses common shaft spinning failure
- **Torque Rated:** Withstands high-torque loads
- **Warranty Covered:** Manufacturer defect protection

## Known Issues and Solutions

### Issue: Controller Compatibility
- **Note:** Current production uses MT60 connectors
- **Solution:** Ensure VESC has matching connectors or use adapters
- **Legacy:** Older motors used different connectors

### Issue: Shaft Wear
- **Symptoms:** Keyway wear, pulley loosening
- **Solution:** Use steel pulleys, check tightness regularly
- **Prevention:** Threadlocker on fasteners

### Issue: Vent Hole Debris
- **Symptoms:** Dirt ingress through CNC holes
- **Solution:** Install mesh screens for off-road use
- **Maintenance:** Regular cleaning
