# QS Motor 205 and 273 Series Hub Motors

## Overview

QS Motor (also known as Taizhou QS Motor Co., Ltd. or MQCON) is a leading Chinese manufacturer of high-power brushless DC hub motors for electric motorcycles, e-bikes, and EV applications. Their QS205 and QS273 series are legendary in the DIY EV community for their robust construction, high power capability, and excellent VESC compatibility. These motors are true "sleeper" performers capable of handling 5-10x their rated power briefly.

**Manufacturer:** Taizhou QS Motor Co., Ltd. / MQCON (Taizhou, China)  
**Official Website:** https://www.qsmotor.cc  
**Primary Applications:** Electric motorcycles, high-power e-bikes, EV conversions, go-karts

## Performance Characteristics

### Power Handling (Real World)
| Motor | Rated | Safe Continuous | Brief Peak | Extreme (Risky) |
|-------|-------|-----------------|------------|-----------------|
| QS205 50H | 3000W | 5000W | 8000W | 12000W |
| QS273 40H | 4000W | 6000W | 10000W | 15000W |
| QS273 50H | 8000W | 12000W | 20000W | 30000W |

**Warnings:**
- Peak power requires adequate cooling
- Continuous high power requires thermal monitoring
- Phase current more critical than battery current
- Magnet demagnetization risk at excessive temperatures

### Heat Management
- **Hub Motors:** Heat dissipation through rim/tire
- **Problem:** QS205/273 can overheat in sustained high-power use
- **Solutions:**
  - HubSinks (aluminum heat sink fins on motor cover)
  - Statorade (ferrofluid in air gap)
  - Forced air cooling
  - Thermal rollback in VESC

### Efficiency
- **Peak:** 90%+ at optimal load
- **Typical E-Bike:** 85-88%
- **High Power:** Drops as heat increases

### Cogging and Startup
- Large hub motors have significant cogging torque
- Hall sensors essential for smooth startup
- Some VESC users report hall sensor noise issues
- Solutions: Ferrite beads on hall wires, proper grounding

## VESC Configuration Guide

### QS205-50H 3T (11.9 kV) Example
```
Motor Type: FOC
Pole Pairs: 16

Current Limits:
  Motor Max: 100A (thermal limited)
  Motor Min: -100A (regen)
  Battery Max: 60A (conservative) or 100A (aggressive)
  Battery Min: -20A (regen)
  
Voltage:
  Min: 40V
  Max: 84V (20S - verify insulation rating)
  
Sensorless:
  ERPM Sensorless: 1500-2000 (large motor, high kV)
  
Hall Sensors:
  Enabled for startup
  Sensorless transition: ~2000 ERPM
  
Temperature:
  Sensor Type: KTY83-122
  Cutoff Start: 100°C
  Cutoff End: 130°C
```

## Known Issues and Solutions

### Issue: Overheating
- **Symptoms:** Thermal rollback, magnet damage, reduced performance
- **Solution:** HubSinks, Statorade, thermal management
- **Prevention:** Don't sustain max power, monitor temps

### Issue: Cogging on Startup
- **Symptoms:** Jerky start, VESC faults
- **Cause:** Large motor + hall sensor noise
- **Solution:** Ferrite beads on hall wires, sensor/sensorless hybrid mode
- **Workaround:** Push start, higher sensorless ERPM threshold

### Issue: Axle Spin in Dropouts
- **Symptoms:** Motor rotates in frame, damaged wiring
- **Solution:** Torque arms (essential!), proper axle nuts
- **Prevention:** Never run without torque arms on high power

### Issue: Hall Sensor Failures
- **Cause:** Vibration, water ingress, poor connections
- **Solution:** QS has dual sets; can switch to backup set
- **Workaround:** Sensorless mode (if VESC capable)

### Issue: Counterfeit/Clone Motors
- **Risk:** Lower quality magnets, thinner wires
- **Identification:** Buy from verified sources
- **Verification:** Contact QS Motor with serial number
