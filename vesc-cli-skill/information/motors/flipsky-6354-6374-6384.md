# Flipsky 6354/6374/6384 Series Motors

## Overview

Flipsky Technology is a Shenzhen-based company specializing in VESC-compatible electronics for electric skateboards, e-bikes, eFoils, and robotics. Their "Battle Hardened" motor series has become extremely popular in the DIY community due to excellent VESC integration, competitive pricing, and robust construction with pre-epoxied magnets.

**Manufacturer:** Flipsky Technology Co., Ltd. (Shenzhen, China)  
**Official Website:** https://flipsky.net  
**Primary Applications:** Electric skateboards, e-bikes, eFoils, robots, combat bots

## Performance Characteristics

### Efficiency
- **Peak Efficiency:** 85-90% (FOC mode with VESC)
- **High Load Efficiency:** Better than budget motors due to quality laminations
- **No-Load Current:** Low (indicates quality bearings)

### Heat Management
- Battle hardened versions have better heat tolerance
- Steel cooling mesh on some versions (C4/C5)
- Recommended max operating temp: 100°C
- 6374-190KV runs cooler than 6354-190KV due to larger mass

### Noise and Vibration
- "Convex magnets array" designed to reduce FOC noise
- Full paste magnets with epoxy reduce vibration
- Sensored startup eliminates cogging

### Reliability Notes
- **Pre-Battle Hardened:** All Flipsky "Battle Hardened" motors come with factory-epoxied magnets
- **Bearing Quality:** NMB bearings in 6354, upgraded bearings in larger motors
- **Shaft Design:** 420 material with surface hardening
- **Known Issues:**
  - Early versions had sensor wire fragility (improved in recent batches)
  - Some reports of set screw loosening (use threadlocker)
  - Quality control variance between batches
