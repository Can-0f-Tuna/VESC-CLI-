# 21700 Battery Cell Database

## Overview

21700 cells are the next-generation cylindrical lithium-ion format, offering significantly higher capacity and power than 18650 cells. The format was pioneered by Tesla and Panasonic and is now widely adopted for electric vehicles and high-performance applications.

**Cell Naming Convention:**
- **21** = diameter in mm
- **70** = length in mm (nominal)
- **0** = cylindrical format

**Advantages over 18650:**
- ~50% more capacity per cell
- Higher discharge capability
- Better energy density (Wh/kg)
- Fewer cells needed for same capacity
- Reduced pack complexity

- M50LT: Updated chemistry with improved cycle life

## VESC Compatibility Notes

### ERPM Considerations

Higher voltage packs (12S) increase motor ERPM:

```
ERPM = Voltage × Motor KV × (Pole Pairs)

Example for 12S (50.4V max) with 190KV motor (7 pole pairs):
ERPM = 50.4 × 190 × 7 = 67,032
```

**VESC Limits:**
- V4 hardware: 60,000 ERPM limit
- V6 hardware: 150,000 ERPM limit

**Recommendation:**
- For 12S: Use 170KV or lower motors with V4
- For 10S: 190KV motors work well
- For 12S+V4: 170KV or 140KV recommended

### Current Requirements

**Typical E-Skateboard Current Draws:**
- Casual cruising: 10-20A per motor
- Aggressive acceleration: 50-80A per motor
- Hill climbing: 40-60A sustained per motor

**21700 Pack Advantage:**
- 12S2P with P42A: 90A pack current (45A per motor continuous)
- With duty cycle: Supports 60-70A motor currents
- Excellent for dual-drive setups

## Temperature Management

### Operating Temperature Limits

| Cell | Charge °C | Discharge °C | Max Surface Temp |
|------|-----------|--------------|------------------|
| Samsung 40T | 0 to 45 | -20 to 60 | 80°C (cutoff) |
| Molicel P42A | 0 to 60 | -40 to 60 | 60°C (recommended) |
| LG M50LT | 0 to 45 | -20 to 55 | 70°C (BMU cutoff) |
| Samsung 50E | 0 to 45 | -20 to 60 | 60°C |

### Thermal Notes

**P42A Cold Weather Performance:**
- Maintains good discharge down to -40°C
- Capacity retention at -20°C: ~80%+
- Best 21700 for cold climates

**M50LT Temperature Derating:**
- Automatically derates above 25°C ambient
- BMS temperature monitoring essential
- Not ideal for hot climates or high sustained loads

**40T Thermal Performance:**
- Good balance across temperature range
- 80°C surface cutoff protects cell
- Performs well in most conditions

## Technical Notes

### Chemistry Details

**NMC (Nickel Manganese Cobalt):**
- Used in: 40T, P42A, most 21700 cells
- Balanced energy density and power
- Good thermal stability
- Market standard

**High-Nickel NMC:**
- Used in: M50LT
- Higher energy density
- More sensitive to temperature
- Requires careful thermal management

### Manufacturing Quality
All cells listed are from Tier-1 manufacturers with:
- Consistent quality control
- Full traceability
- Proper safety certifications
- Published datasheets

**Avoid:** Unbranded or "rewrapped" 21700 cells claiming specifications beyond these established models.
