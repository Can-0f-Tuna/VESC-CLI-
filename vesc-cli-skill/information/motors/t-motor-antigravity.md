# T-Motor Antigravity Series

## Overview

T-Motor is a leading manufacturer of high-quality brushless motors for UAVs, drones, and increasingly for electric vehicles and robotics. The Antigravity series represents their ultra-lightweight, high-efficiency motors designed for multi-rotor applications. While primarily designed for drones, these motors have found applications in lightweight e-skate builds, robotics, and other projects where weight savings and efficiency are paramount.

**Manufacturer:** T-MOTOR (Nanchang, China)  
**Official Website:** https://uav-en.tmotor.com  
**Primary Applications:** UAVs, VTOL, electric skateboards (lightweight), robotics, aeromodeling

## VESC Compatibility

**Important Note:** T-Motor Antigravity motors are designed for drone ESCs, not VESC. For VESC use:

### Challenges
1. **Low kV Options:** 380KV is high for e-skate direct drive
2. **Small Shaft:** 4mm shaft requires adaptation
3. **No Hall Sensors:** Sensorless only (most models)
4. **Voltage Range:** 6S maximum (most models)
5. **Connector Type:** May require soldering adaptation

### Potential Applications with VESC

#### Lightweight Robotics
- Excellent for small robots
- Precise speed control with FOC
- Low weight reduces robot power requirements

#### Geared E-Skate
- Use with significant gear reduction
- High RPM suitable for small pulleys
- 6S battery systems for lightweight boards

#### Experimental Builds
- Testing efficiency-focused designs
- Learning FOC tuning on low-power systems

### VESC Configuration (if used)
```
Motor Type: FOC (sensorless)
Pole Pairs: 12 (for 24P motors)

Current Limits:
  Motor Max: 16A (limited by motor rating)
  Motor Min: -16A
  Battery Max: 15A
  Battery Min: -5A

Voltage:
  Max: 25.2V (6S)
  
Sensorless:
  ERPM: 2000+ (very high kV)
  
Note: Very high ERPM requires careful configuration
```

## E-Skate Adaptation Guide

### Using T-Motor for E-Skate
While not designed for e-skate, possible with modifications:

#### Required Adaptations
1. **Shaft Extension/Adapter**
   - 4mm to 8mm adapter needed
   - Must handle torque without slipping
   - Consider custom CNC adapter

2. **Gear Reduction**
   - High kV requires significant reduction
   - 5:1 to 10:1 recommended
   - Small motor pulley (10-12T)

3. **Battery Voltage**
   - Limited to 6S (25.2V max)
   - Lower voltage = lower top speed
   - Higher current for same power

4. **Mounting**
   - 16/19mm pattern needs adapter
   - 3D printed or aluminum bracket

#### Potential Build
| Component | Specification |
|-----------|-------------|
| Motor | 2x MN4006-380KV |
| Gear Ratio | 8:1 |
| ESC | 2x FSESC4.12 (sensorless) |
| Battery | 6S 4P |
| Expected Speed | ~25 km/h |
| Weight Savings | ~1.5kg vs 6374 motors |

**Use Case:** Ultralight commuter board, not performance

## Summary

**Best Use Cases:**
- ✅ UAVs and drones (primary application)
- ✅ Lightweight robotics
- ✅ Experimental efficiency-focused builds
- ✅ Learning motor control on low-power systems

**Not Recommended For:**
- ❌ Standard electric skateboards (without significant adaptation)
- ❌ High-power applications
- ❌ Direct drive e-skate (kV too high)
- ❌ Heavy riders or hill climbing

**Bottom Line:** T-Motor Antigravity motors are exceptional for their intended UAV applications. While they can be adapted for ground vehicles, they are not practical substitutes for purpose-built e-skate motors like 63xx series without significant compromises.
