# Maytech 6364/6374/6384 Series Motors

## Overview

Maytech is a leading Chinese manufacturer of brushless motors for electric skateboards, e-bikes, and robotics. Their 63mm outrunner series (6364, 6374, 6384) are among the most widely used motors in the DIY electric skateboard community. These motors are known for their reliability, quality construction, and excellent VESC compatibility.

**Manufacturer:** Maytech Electric Co., Ltd. (Shenzhen, China)  
**Official Website:** https://maytech.cn  
**Primary Applications:** Electric skateboards, e-bikes, robotics, combat robots, electric vehicles

## Performance Characteristics

### Efficiency
- **Peak Efficiency:** 85-90% (varies by load)
- **Efficiency at 50% Load:** ~88%
- **No-Load Current:** 0.8A (indicates quality bearings and low friction)

### Heat Management
- Max operating temperature: 100°C (recommended stay below 80°C)
- Sealed versions run slightly hotter due to reduced airflow
- Stainless steel cooling mesh versions offer best heat dissipation with protection

### Noise Level
- Low cogging torque design
- Smooth operation in both sensored and sensorless modes
- 6374-170HA noted for quieter operation than 190kV variants

### Reliability Notes
- **Hall Sensor Reliability:** V4+ versions have hall sensors embedded in stator for improved accuracy and durability
- **Magnet Retention:** Factory epoxy bonding; battle-hardened versions available
- **Bearing Life:** NSK bearings typically last 1000+ hours with proper maintenance
- **Common Issues:** 
  - Early versions had hall sensor PCB failures (addressed in V4+)
  - Shaft set screws can loosen without threadlocker
  - Sealed versions may overheat in sustained high-load applications

## Known Issues and Solutions

### Issue: Hall Sensor Failures
- **Symptoms:** Cogging at startup, motor jerking, VESC fault codes
- **Solution:** Upgrade to V4+ models with embedded hall sensors; verify wiring integrity
- **Prevention:** Secure sensor cables, avoid tension on connectors

### Issue: Magnet Loosening
- **Symptoms:** Grinding noise, vibration, reduced power
- **Solution:** Epoxy magnets (battle hardening), avoid overheating
- **Prevention:** Monitor motor temperature, add temp sensor to VESC

### Issue: Shaft Slippage
- **Symptoms:** Pulley spinning on shaft, loss of power transmission
- **Solution:** Use threadlocker on set screws, D-shaft versions preferred
- **Prevention:** Regularly check fastener tightness

### Issue: Sealed Motor Overheating
- **Symptoms:** Motor hot to touch, thermal throttling
- **Solution:** Use cooling mesh versions instead of fully sealed
- **Prevention:** Monitor temps, reduce continuous load, add external cooling
